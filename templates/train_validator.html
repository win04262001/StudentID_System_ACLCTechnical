How to Train the Model
  1. Upload at least 5 examples of valid ID photos (clear face, white background, etc.)
  2. Upload at least 5 examples of invalid ID photos (blurry, no face, busy background, etc.)
  3. Click the "Train Model" button to create a machine learning model
  4. Test the model with new photos to see if it correctly identifies them
  5. Add more training examples if the model makes mistakes
