Photo Requirements

This validator uses machine learning to determine if a photo is suitable for a student ID. The model has been trained on examples of acceptable and unacceptable photos.

General guidelines:

  • Clear, focused image (not extremely blurry)
  • Plain white or light-colored background
  • Face should be clearly visible
  • Only one person in the photo
