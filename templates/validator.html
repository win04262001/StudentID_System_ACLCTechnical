Photo Requirements

  • High resolution (minimum 300x300 pixels)
  • Clear, focused image (not blurry)
  • Plain white or light-colored background
  • Neutral facial expression
  • Face centered and not tilted
  • No hats, sunglasses, or accessories
  • Face should take up 10-70% of the image
